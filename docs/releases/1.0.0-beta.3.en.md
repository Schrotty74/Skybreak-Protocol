# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.0-beta.3

This bugfix beta addresses the reported desktop problems in the previous beta and gives all ten levels a clearly distinct visual identity. It focuses on a more reliable Ultra mode, a dependable music switch, and substantially more varied game worlds.

## Ultra mode on Mac

- Safari and Chrome now use a transparent WebGPU effect layer on desktop Macs.
- The unreliable copy of the complete game canvas into a second WebGPU texture has been removed from this path.
- The original 2D game image therefore remains visible as the base layer at all times.
- This avoids the previously black or heavily darkened Ultra presentation.

## Firefox performance

- The Firefox Ultra fallback uses native alpha composition instead of an expensive full-canvas CSS blend mode.
- The game and effect paths are capped at 30 FPS when Firefox uses the WebGL2 fallback.
- Internal effect resolution remains reduced to lower load and severe stutter.
- Unaffected devices retain their existing WebGPU or compatible rendering path.

## Music control

- `MUSIC OFF` no longer pauses only the most recently started track.
- A previous track that is still playing during a level crossfade is now stopped completely.
- Pending playback requests are invalidated when music is switched off.
- Music and sound effects remain independently configurable.

## Ten distinct level worlds

- **Neon Undercity:** service shafts, pipes, rain, and fast maglev traffic.
- **Chrome Bazaar:** floating lanterns, market stalls, and animated advertising.
- **Toxic Transit:** tunnel ribs, moving train windows, and rising gas bubbles.
- **Crimson Firewall:** pulsing data walls, firewall columns, and flying embers.
- **Azure Data Sea:** layered waves, bubbles, and luminous data jellyfish.
- **Violet Reactor:** rotating reactor rings, plasma arcs, and an unstable energy core.
- **Solar Megagrid:** a blazing sun, heat shimmer, and moving solar-panel arrays.
- **Ghost Network:** flickering phantom nodes, packet streams, and glitch effects.
- **Quantum Rift:** a rotating singularity, spiral arms, and warped star trails.
- **Skybreak Apex:** dawn above the clouds, transmission rays, and the final tower.

## Platforms and presentation

- Every level now has its own platform material with matching colors and surface patterns.
- Shared city, traffic, advertising, rain, and grid effects are drawn only in levels where they fit the environment.
- Individual backdrops are no longer obscured by the same general city scene.
- The German and English PDF manuals have been updated with the new level descriptions.

## Offline use, updates, and privacy

- The offline ZIP has been rebuilt for `1.0.0-beta.3` and still contains all ten music tracks plus starters for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
- The update checker detects this beta through the public GitHub release.
- No new network requests, accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking were added.
- Existing local saves, unlocked levels, difficulties, and key bindings remain intact.

## Known beta limitations

- Ultra performance still depends on browser, operating system, graphics driver, and device.
- Firefox deliberately uses a lighter Ultra fallback than browsers with working WebGPU.
- Hardware ray tracing is not directly available through the WebGPU features currently in use.
