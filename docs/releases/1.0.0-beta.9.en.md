# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.0-beta.9

This beta makes the ten worlds clearly distinct in gameplay as well as appearance. It adds individual platform rules, objectives, enemy roles, and guardian attacks to the levels.

## Individual rules per level

- The ten levels now combine fracture zones, moving and icy platforms, side currents, firewall lasers, energy pulses, phased platforms, and rift jump fields.
- Energy cells or access switches must be activated before leaving a level.
- Rift jumps move the robot between active rift platforms; invisible phased platforms do not support it.

## Enemies, guardians, and ice pick

- Patrol, leap attack, and direction change are visibly distinct enemy archetypes in every world. Shield, shooter, and hover enemies add encounters.
- Every guardian uses a distinct projectile pattern with its own direction, speed, count, and trajectory. The pattern accelerates during the final integrity stage.
- The ice pick activates switches, dissolves enemy projectiles, and breaks shields. A first hit freezes normal enemies; the next disables them.
- From power level 4, an attack in mid-air briefly creates an ice bridge.

## Manuals and privacy

- The German and English PDF manuals document the new platforms, objectives, hazards, enemy roles, ice-pick functions, and the concrete rule of every level.
- No accounts, analytics services, trackers, new browser-storage values, additional network requests, or external content were added.

## Known beta limitations

- The new level rules are built and checked, but still need complete manual runs on desktop and mobile devices for fairness, collisions, and thermal behavior.
- Frame rate and heat still depend on the device, browser, battery level, ambient temperature, graphics level, and render resolution.
