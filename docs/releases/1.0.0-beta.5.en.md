# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.0-beta.5

This beta improves progression through the ten sectors and makes local macOS testing available with a double-click. Roaming power-up chests now remain understandable and visible at higher difficulties, while enemies turn reliably at the side boundaries.

## Difficulty and level progression

- The difficulty selected for a level is automatically used as the default for the immediately following level after successful completion.
- Graphics quality, render resolution, controls, and all other settings remain unchanged.
- This only affects the next unlocked sector and does not alter any previously saved personal settings.

## Roaming power-up chests

- A roaming chest that has already appeared remains available when the robot briefly jumps to a lower row and then returns.
- On **Hard**, relocated roaming chests spawn inside the visible camera range, preferably at the robot's height or above it.
- On **Ultra**, the intentionally time-limited behavior remains: when the timer expires, the chest is placed at a new visible position.

## Stability and local testing

- Enemies now turn around reliably at the left and right boundaries, including when a high velocity or a longer frame interval moves them past an edge.
- `Skybreak-Protocol-Lokaltest.command` starts the local development test channel on macOS with a double-click, or opens it when it is already running.
- The starter runs only the local Vite development build and does not publish any data.

## Documentation, offline play, and privacy

- Both PDF manuals explain the carried-over difficulty and the behavior of roaming chests.
- The offline ZIP continues to include all music tracks and starters for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
- No accounts, analytics services, advertising, trackers, or additional network requests were added.

## Known beta limitations

- The effect of **Ultra** and the frame rate still depend on the browser and the hardware in use.
- The local macOS starter requires the project-local Node dependencies; after a fresh checkout, run `npm ci` in the project folder first.
