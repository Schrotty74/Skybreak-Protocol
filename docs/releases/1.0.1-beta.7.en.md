# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1-beta.7

This beta makes gameplay rules consistent across all fourteen levels and improves the readability of phase and moving platforms.

## Consistent difficulty

- Easy uses four patrols and one guardian in every level, Medium five patrols and two guardians, and Hard six patrols and two guardians.
- Enemy movement, patrol and guardian projectiles, bombs, jumps, falling hazards, and their timing now depend only on the selected difficulty, not on the level number.
- The former extra laser, pulse, wind, heat, and water gameplay effects no longer differentiate levels. Scenery, music, colour palette, and platform appearance remain distinct.

## Platforms and boss arena

- Phase blocks are placed on separate floors: four on Easy, eight on Medium, and twelve on Hard. They never sit next to one another and support the robot only while active.
- Every phase block has a high-contrast frame, scan lines, and a `PHASE 01` through `PHASE 14` marker so it remains readable in every environment.
- Sideways-moving routes are also distributed across the full ascent: four on Easy, six on Medium, and eight on Hard.
- Medium and Hard start every level with two bosses. Guardians turn at the outer boss-arena edge but can still fall through genuine gaps.

## Documentation and privacy

- The German and English READMEs plus both PDF manuals document the consistent difficulty values and new platform distribution.
- Progress, unlocks, settings, and performance data remain local to the browser. No accounts, trackers, analytics services, or new network access were added.

Real-device testing on desktop and mobile remains useful, especially for phase-marker readability and the balance of denser platform routes.
