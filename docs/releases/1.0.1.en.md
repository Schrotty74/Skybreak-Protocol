# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1

Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1 is the first stable release of the vertical cyberpunk arcade game for modern desktop and mobile browsers. It promotes the verified beta baseline without introducing untested gameplay systems and brings together fourteen distinct ascents, local saves, an offline package, and separate build channels.

## Fourteen distinct ascents

- Every level has 15 floors, its own 2.5D scenery, fair route variants, platform appearance, objective devices, guardian arena, and music.
- Levels 1 to 10 use ten different sample-free retro arcade arrangements; elemental Levels 11 to 14 retain their own tracks.
- Sector labels describe the environment only and no longer imply removed wind, laser, or pulse effects.

## Consistent, understandable difficulty

- Easy uses four patrols and one guardian in every level, Medium five patrols and two guardians, and Hard six patrols and two guardians. Only selected patrols shoot; Medium and Hard stagger those shots.
- Guardian integrity, shields, chests, walls, double decks, and falling hazards depend only on the selected difficulty. Falling objects visibly detach from existing walkways rather than the top of the screen.
- Phase blocks and sideways-moving routes are distributed across every ascent and remain clearly readable: four/eight/twelve phase blocks and four/six/eight moving routes on Easy/Medium/Hard.

## Stability, offline play, and privacy

- Music management clears earlier audio elements on level transitions, restart, pause, menu changes, and unmounting, so an older track cannot overlap a new level track.
- The versioned offline ZIP includes the web app, launchers for macOS, Windows, and Linux, and all fourteen music tracks.
- Progress, unlocks, key bindings, graphics, and game settings remain local to the browser. This release adds no accounts, trackers, analytics services, or new network access.
