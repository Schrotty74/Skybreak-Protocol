# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1-beta.3

This beta expands graphics and performance control, improves the presentation of the game world, and adds cosmetic selection options.

## Graphics, frame rate, and benchmark

- Every graphics preset – Low, Medium, High, and Ultra – now offers the same choice of 60 FPS, up to 120 FPS, or Unlimited.
- The new local benchmark uses the same demanding scene for 14 seconds after a two-second warm-up. It reports graphics preset, resolution, frame-rate limit, FPS, frame time, and CPU time for updating and drawing.
- Safari on a 120 Hz display can render above 60 FPS after disabling “Prefer Page Rendering Updates near 60fps” in Feature Flags. A Mac Studio M4 Max reached 120 FPS at 4K Ultra.
- Ultra uses prepared sprites, cached background layers, visibility culling, and GPU-instanced highlights so the detailed scene creates less repeated Canvas work.

## Game world and selection

- Platforms now read more spatially through hovering 2.5D modules, level-specific forms, material cores, and break patterns; destruction uses matching energy, water, wind, magma, or mineral explosions.
- Every level uses its own robot type. Unlocked robot models can then be selected independently of the level; the cosmetic hologram avatar remains available.
- The start view groups level selection with its difficulty. Easy, Medium, and Hard are stored locally for each level.
- Chests continue to offer seven clearly selectable rewards, and falling hazards deal damage again.

## Documentation and privacy

- German and English front pages plus both PDF manuals document the frame-rate limits, benchmark, and Safari 120 Hz instruction.
- No accounts, trackers, analytics services, new network access, or private data were added. The frame-rate choice and cosmetic selections are additionally stored only locally in the browser; all performance values remain local.

Complete testing on different desktop browsers and real mobile devices remains necessary for visuals, frame rate, thermal behaviour, and battery life.
