# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.0-beta.8

This beta makes level differences more visible and readable, and makes the mobile performance display directly controllable.

## Enemies, guardians, and ice pick

- Regular enemies and guardians have their own silhouette, sensor form, movement accents, and color world in each of the ten levels.
- The ice pick uses its own base model in every level's color world. Style upgrades remain visible as a core and glow accent.
- After completing a level, the upgrade selection now shows the concrete next power level with reach and breakable platform modules per strike, plus the next design with its name, color, and shape.

## Local FPS display

- On mobile devices, the live display is clearly readable in the black header during active runs and shows FPS plus frame time.
- The display can be enabled or disabled on mobile and desktop with FPS ON/OFF; the choice remains only in local browser storage.

## Privacy and offline mode

- The documentation now fully lists internal render resolution and FPS-display visibility as two additional non-personal browser settings.
- No accounts, analytics services, trackers, additional network requests, or external content were added.
- The offline ZIP continues to include all music tracks and starters for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## Known beta limitations

- Achievable frame rate and heat still depend on device, browser, battery level, ambient temperature, and graphics level.
- On an iPhone 17 Pro, Ultra reaches 60 FPS at 1080p; 4K Ultra is not a 60 FPS target mode on mobile devices.
