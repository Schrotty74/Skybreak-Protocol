# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.0-beta.7

This beta makes performance directly visible on real mobile devices. During an active run, touch devices show a compact live indicator over the game field. It lets players check frame rate and average frame time in High or Ultra without crowding the mobile header or the controls.

## Mobile performance indicator

- The new indicator appears only on touch devices and only during an active run.
- It shows actual rendered frames per second plus average frame time, for example `60 FPS · 16.7 MS`.
- The indicator is not interactive, does not cover the touch controls, and disappears outside an active run.
- Desktop retains its existing, more detailed live readout.

## Testing and privacy

- The indicator helps with real-device checks of frame rate, heat, and graphics levels on iPhone and Android devices.
- Saves, settings, and measurements remain local. No accounts, analytics services, trackers, or additional network requests were added.
- The offline ZIP continues to include all music tracks and starters for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## Known beta limitations

- Achievable frame rates and heat still depend on device, browser, battery level, ambient temperature, and selected graphics level.
- The local macOS starter requires the project-local Node dependencies. After a fresh checkout, run `npm ci` in the project folder.
