# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1-beta.2

This beta expands Skybreak Protocol from ten to fourteen complete levels and refreshes the presentation of the entire game world.

## Fourteen levels and elemental sectors

- The four new 15-floor levels are Inferno Foundry, Abyssal Data Ocean, Stratosphere Relay, and Terra Core Citadel.
- Every elemental sector has its own backdrop, platform shape, material core, guardian attacks, block destruction, animated environment, and dedicated 48-second music track.
- Fire shows irregular heat plumes and brief lift during visible heat surges. Water uses manta rays, a visible wet film, and temporary reduced grip. Air shows wind gusts with a slight horizontal influence. Earth uses levitating stones and visible quakes.

## Game world and rewards

- Every platform is now a hovering 2.5D module with shadow, depth, and level-specific surfaces. Inner patterns and break marks also differ per world.
- Block destruction uses matching energy, water, wind, magma, or mineral effects on High and Ultra.
- Chests now let players choose from seven rewards: shield, life, data bonus, overdrive, jackpot, repair plus shield, and phase armor.
- The new guardian logic for the elemental levels uses visible attacks without the distracting repeated Guardian Attack notification.

## Audio, manuals, and publication

- The new levels use real, normalized MP3 tracks, each 48 seconds long with melody, harmony, bass, and rhythm. This removes blinking one-shot audio sessions and synthetic continuous tones.
- German and English manuals plus both GitHub front pages now cover fourteen levels, fourteen tracks, elemental mechanics, and expanded chest rewards.
- No accounts, trackers, analytics services, additional network requests, or browser-storage values were added.
- Full manual desktop and mobile testing remains necessary for fairness, collisions, frame rate, thermal behaviour, and battery life.
