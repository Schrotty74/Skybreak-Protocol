# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1-beta.1

This beta consolidates the recently expanded game rules, completion presentation, and manuals. It also corrects the cosmetic bikini avatar after a completed level.

## Onboarding, rules, and completion

- Easy is documented as a complete onboarding mode: eight lives, no regular enemies, one required objective, stable platforms, and no drafts or environmental hazards. The guardian needs only one hit and attacks more slowly.
- After a level goal, the robot shows a five-second SKYBREAK DANCE sequence before the upgrade or finale. The cosmetic bikini avatar instead shows its five-second look presentation.
- The additional bikini-avatar cheat is enabled during an active run by switching MUSIC OFF/ON twice within five seconds. It is cosmetic only, applies to the current run, and changes neither gameplay values nor records.

## Avatar-presentation correction

- The geometric forms previously drawn over the bikini asset have been removed. The outfit and figure now remain fully visible during the completion presentation.
- The transparent avatar asset is drawn at its natural aspect ratio and is not distorted beyond the available screen bounds.

## Manuals, privacy, and known limitations

- The German and English PDF manuals explain the onboarding mode, dance sequence, cosmetic cheat, and level-specific look presentation.
- No accounts, trackers, analytics services, additional network requests, or new browser-storage values were added.
- The look presentation still uses the existing figure asset. Genuine new outfit and pose artwork for each level requires separately supplied, usable assets.
- Full manual desktop and mobile testing remains necessary for fairness, collisions, frame rate, thermal behaviour, and battery life.
