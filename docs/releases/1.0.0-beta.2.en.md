# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.0-beta.2

The second public beta expands Skybreak Protocol with power-up chests, secret cheats, persistent level selection, remappable desktop controls, and targeted Ultra fixes. The documentation and offline build have been updated for the new release.

## Power-ups and chests

- Wooden chests contain a shield, an extra life, a score bonus, or twelve seconds of ice-pick overdrive.
- Shields, overdrive, opened chests, and power-up messages are shown directly in the game.
- On **Easy**, permanent chests appear every two to three platform floors.
- On **Medium**, chests unlock halfway through the sector, remain for eight seconds, and then relocate.
- On **Hard**, chests unlock after two thirds of the sector, remain for 4.5 seconds, and relocate faster, including to positions below the player.

## Secret cheats

- Four secret action sequences activate level-bound immortality, a double shield, 30 seconds of overdrive, or an extra life.
- Cheat input is armed by activating the SP symbol five times and works with both touch and keyboard controls.
- The SP symbol must be activated five times again before each additional code.
- Runs that use a cheat do not update the locally stored high score.

## Level progression and controls

- Reached levels are unlocked locally and remain available in the browser.
- Any unlocked level can be selected as the starting point from the ready, defeat, and victory screens.
- A completed run can therefore continue directly with the next unlocked level instead of only repeating the previous one.
- Left, right, jump, and attack controls can be remapped on desktop.
- Duplicate assignments are swapped automatically, and the default `A`, `D`, `Space`, and `X` layout can be restored at any time.

## Ultra graphics and mobile safety

- The Ultra screen now uses transparent blending on desktop as well, preventing an empty WebGPU frame from darkening the base game in Safari or Chrome.
- The WebGL2 Ultra fallback is capped at 40 FPS and a medium internal effect resolution to reduce severe stutter, especially in Firefox.
- A prominent in-game and project warning explains that Ultra can make phones very warm.
- Ultra use on phones is discouraged in hot weather or direct sunlight.

## Documentation and presentation

- The previous mobile screenshots were replaced with current start-screen and Ultra-gameplay captures.
- Separate eight-page German and English PDF manuals explain gameplay, every button, levels, settings, power-ups, and cheat codes.
- The manuals use the game artwork, icon, and compact screenshots and are linked from the matching README.
- The release workflow now requires both manuals to be updated and visually reviewed after gameplay-related changes.

## Offline use, updates, and privacy

- The offline ZIP has been updated for `1.0.0-beta.2` and still includes all ten music tracks plus starters for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
- The in-game update check detects this beta through the public GitHub release.
- Level unlocks and custom key bindings are stored only in the local browser.
- No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking were added.

## Known beta limitations

- Ultra rendering still depends on the browser, operating system, and graphics driver; unsupported devices use a compatible renderer.
- Hardware ray tracing is not directly available through the WebGPU features currently in use.
- The update check requires internet access; save data, settings, and key bindings are not transmitted.
