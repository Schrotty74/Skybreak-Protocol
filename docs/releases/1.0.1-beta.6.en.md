# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1-beta.6

This beta expands every ascent with clearly scaled enemy and guardian encounters, new obstacles, and more readable sector-specific hazards.

## Difficulty, enemies, and guardians

- Easy contains four patrol enemies and one guardian, Medium five patrols and two guardians, and Hard six patrols and two guardians.
- Only part of the patrol group can fire. On Medium and Hard, shots are staggered so they do not become an uninterrupted volley.
- Only one guardian per encounter can fire and throw bombs. The second guardian on Medium and Hard remains mobile and dangerous on contact, but cannot create an additional bomb stream.
- Guardian integrity is two hits on Easy, five on Medium, and eight on Hard.

## Routes, hazards, and chests

- Destructible vertical walls extend the platform routes: two on Easy, four on Medium, and six on Hard.
- Double decks are distributed through the ascent and require two deliberate upward jumps before the route opens.
- Sector-specific, readable hazards now detach below walkways - such as ice shards, flames, droplets, crystals, or stone fragments. Their position, fall speed, and count scale with difficulty.
- Easy has five fixed chests, Medium four fixed chests plus two roaming bonus chests after 40% height, and Hard two fixed chests plus one roaming bonus chest after 60% height.
- Roaming chests have their own colour treatment and grant enhanced rewards, including longer shields, longer phase, more overdrive, points, or lives.

## Shield and documentation

- A shield lasts eight seconds and absorbs three hits on Easy, six seconds and two hits on Medium, or four seconds and one hit on Hard.
- German and English landing pages, release documents, and both PDF manuals describe the updated rules. Progress, unlocks, benchmarks, and settings remain local in the browser.

Real-device testing on desktop and mobile remains valuable, especially for the balance of Medium and Hard enemy waves.
