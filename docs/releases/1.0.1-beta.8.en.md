# Skybreak Protocol 1.0.1-beta.8

This beta makes the fourteen levels more distinct without changing the confirmed difficulty values for each setting.

## Unique routes and set pieces

- Every level now uses its own fair route blueprint with equally wide, reachable gaps and three replay variants.
- The route, guardian arena, and two objective devices receive level-specific non-colliding presentation. Patrol count, guardian integrity, shots, falling objects, and their timing still depend only on Easy, Medium, or Hard.
- Sector profiles now use environment-only names such as SERVICE SHAFTS, TRANSIT LINES, and TRANSMISSION TERRACES. They no longer imply removed wind, laser, or pulse effects.

## Music and transitions

- Levels 1 to 10 now use ten original, sample-free retro-arcade arrangements. Tempo, meter, melody, bass pattern, and percussion differ to fit each level.
- Only the current MP3 track remains active during a level transition. Interrupted crossfades and the transition to elemental levels now reliably clean up older audio elements.

## Documentation and privacy

- The German and English READMEs plus both PDF manuals document routes, objective devices, guardian arenas, and the new music.
- No accounts, trackers, analytics services, or new network access were added. Progress and settings remain local to the browser.

Testing on desktop and mobile remains useful, especially for readability of the new route set pieces and rapid consecutive level transitions.
